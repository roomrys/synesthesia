Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click to move the playhead (the frame slice shows that moment); drag the playhead to scrub. Drag the yellow handles to crop: the vertical ones set the time, the horizontal ones on the activations set the lowest and highest notes kept (notes outside are dimmed and not played). [ and ] set the time crop at the playhead, Space plays and pauses, L toggles looping, - and = change speed. Residual: orange is sound the templates don't explain, blue is sound they add that isn't there. Activations: one row per template (percussive sounds at the top); white outlines are candidates, filled boxes are the notes kept, blue ticks are onsets.

Audio stays in this page's memory and is never saved or uploaded.
