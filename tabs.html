2 Tab and crop

0:00.0
Crop –

Each number is a fret, on its string (the top line is the high e string). Hover a note to see everywhere else it could be played, and click one to move it there. Notes you place by hand get a yellow mark and stay put when the rest are placed again. With a note selected: type a fret number, ↑ ↓ move it to the next string with the same pitch, F fixes or unfixes it, Delete removes it. Drag a note sideways to change its time; double-click a string to add a note. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click to jump; the strip at the top is the whole tab. Space plays. Drag the yellow handles to crop: Loop crop (or L) repeats the crop, and only the crop is saved. [ and ] set the crop at the playhead.

3 Save

Saved as a .synesthesia file. Only the notes inside the crop are saved, starting from 0:00; positions you placed by hand are kept.
